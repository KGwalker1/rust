### 🎯 K.C. Montgomery: Exploratory Drive

**1. Core Idea**

- **Definition**: Montgomery proposed that **novel stimuli** automatically elicit an **exploratory drive**. This drive decreases as the organism is exposed to the stimulus over time and recovers when the stimulus is absent .
- **Key Insight**: Exploratory behavior is **not** derived from other drives like hunger, thirst, or general activity. It is a **primary drive** in its own right .

**2. Key Research Findings (Must Know for NET)**

| Finding                                | Study/Evidence                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hunger/Thirst reduces exploration**  | Deprived rats explored significantly less than satiated rats, supporting the view that exploratory drive is separate and can be weakened by other primary drives                   |
| **Activity deprivation has no effect** | Rats confined for 8 days showed no difference in exploration compared to rats with activity wheels—exploration is independent of a "general activity drive"                        |
| **Exploration as reinforcement**       | Rats learned a discrimination task when the reward was simply **1 minute of exploration** in a maze. Reinforcement may involve an **increase**, not decrease, in exploratory drive |

**3. Key Theoretical Points**

- **Drive properties**: The exploratory drive follows a **satiation-recovery cycle**: it is high for novel stimuli, decreases with exposure (satiation), and recovers when the stimulus is absent .
- **Relation to fear**: Montgomery also studied how **novelty-induced fear** interacts with exploratory drive—high fear can suppress exploration .

**4. Criticisms (Quick Points)**

- **Theoretical neutrality**: Montgomery's theory was relatively neutral regarding the S-R vs. cognitive debate, focusing more on empirical facts than grand theory .
- **Ambiguity in reinforcement mechanism**: He suggested reinforcement might involve **drive increase** rather than drive reduction, but did not assert this strongly .

---

**Exam Tip**: Remember **Montgomery = Exploratory Drive**. Link the key findings: **hunger/thirst reduce exploration** (separate drive), **activity deprivation has no effect** (independent of general activity), and **exploration itself can serve as reinforcement**. This is the empirical foundation that Berlyne later built upon theoretically.

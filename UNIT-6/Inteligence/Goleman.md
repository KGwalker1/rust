# **Daniel Goleman's Emotional Intelligence Theory**

## **1. Introduction**

- **Daniel Goleman (born 1946)** – American psychologist, science journalist and author.
- Popularised the concept of **Emotional Intelligence (EI)** through his book _Emotional Intelligence: Why It Can Matter More Than IQ_ (1995).
- His work was influenced by the earlier emotional intelligence model of **Peter Salovey and John D. Mayer (1990)**.
- Goleman emphasised that emotional intelligence contributes to personal, social and professional success.

## **2. Five Components of Emotional Intelligence**

Goleman's original model (1995) identified five major components:

| Component              | Meaning                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Self-awareness**  | Recognising and understanding one's own emotions, strengths and weaknesses. |
| **2. Self-regulation** | Managing emotions and controlling impulsive behaviour.                      |
| **3. Motivation**      | Using emotions to pursue goals with commitment and persistence.             |
| **4. Empathy**         | Understanding and recognising the emotions of others.                       |
| **5. Social skills**   | Managing relationships, communication, cooperation and conflict resolution. |

## **3. Explanation of the Five Components**

1. **Self-awareness:** Knowing how emotions influence thoughts and behaviour.
2. **Self-regulation:** Controlling emotional reactions and responding appropriately.
3. **Motivation:** Maintaining enthusiasm and working towards goals despite difficulties.
4. **Empathy:** Understanding another person's feelings and perspective.
5. **Social skills:** Building relationships, influencing others and working effectively in groups.

## **4. Four-Domain Model**

In his later work, Goleman organised emotional intelligence into four domains:

| Domain                      | Competencies                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Self-awareness**          | Emotional self-awareness                                                        |
| **Self-management**         | Emotional self-control, adaptability, achievement orientation, positive outlook |
| **Social awareness**        | Empathy, organisational awareness                                               |
| **Relationship management** | Influence, coaching and mentoring, conflict management, teamwork                |

**Important:** The original five-component model and the later four-domain model are different presentations of Goleman's framework.

## **5. Salovey and Mayer vs Goleman**

| Salovey and Mayer (1990)                                        | Goleman (1995)                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Introduced the concept of emotional intelligence in psychology. | Popularised emotional intelligence among the general public. |
| Focused on processing emotional information.                    | Emphasised emotional and social competencies.                |
| Ability-based approach.                                         | Broader competency-based approach.                           |

## **6. Importance of Emotional Intelligence**

- Improves interpersonal relationships.
- Helps in emotional regulation and stress management.
- Supports leadership and teamwork.
- Improves conflict resolution.
- Contributes to personal and professional effectiveness.

## **7. Criticisms**

- Some components overlap with personality traits and general social skills.
- Difficulties exist in measuring emotional intelligence objectively.
- The relationship between EI and job performance may be overstated.
- Goleman's broad model is sometimes criticised for mixing abilities, personality characteristics and motivational traits.

## **9. Common MCQ Traps**

- **Salovey and Mayer (1990)** introduced the psychological model of emotional intelligence; Goleman popularised it in 1995.
- **Empathy** means understanding others' emotions, not controlling one's own emotions.
- **Self-awareness** means recognising one's own emotions.
- **Self-regulation** means managing one's emotional responses.
- Goleman's original model has **five components**, whereas his later framework has **four domains**.

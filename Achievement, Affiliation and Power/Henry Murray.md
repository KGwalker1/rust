# **Henry Murray – Personology and Need Theory**

UGC-NET JRF Psychology | Quick Revision Notes

## **1. Introduction**

- **Henry Alexander Murray (1893–1988):** American psychologist known for developing **Personology**, a comprehensive theory of personality.
- Worked at Harvard University and helped establish the Harvard Psychological Clinic (1927).
- Collaborated with **Christiana Morgan** in developing the Thematic Apperception Test (TAT).
- Major work: _Explorations in Personality_ (1938), with collaborators including Morgan.

**NET association:** Murray → Personology + Needs + Press + TAT.

## **2. Personology**

**Personology:** Murray's approach to understanding the whole person through personality, needs, environmental influences and life experiences.

- Personality is dynamic and continuously developing.
- Behaviour results from the interaction between internal needs and environmental pressures.
- Individual personality must be understood as an integrated whole.

## **3. Theory of Needs**

Murray proposed that human behaviour is motivated by psychological needs.

### **Types of needs**

| Type                   | Meaning                           |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Viscerogenic needs** | Biological or physiological needs |
| **Psychogenic needs**  | Psychological or social needs     |

Examples of psychogenic needs:

| Need                    | Meaning                                          |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Achievement (n Ach)** | Desire to succeed and accomplish difficult tasks |
| **Affiliation (n Aff)** | Desire for friendship and social relationships   |
| **Power (n Pow)**       | Desire to influence or control others            |
| **Autonomy**            | Desire for independence                          |
| **Dominance**           | Desire to control one's environment or others    |
| **Exhibition**          | Desire to attract attention                      |
| **Aggression**          | Desire to overcome opposition or retaliate       |
| **Nurturance**          | Desire to help and protect others                |
| **Order**               | Desire for organisation and arrangement          |
| **Understanding**       | Desire for knowledge and explanation             |

**NET point:** Achievement, affiliation and power are particularly important needs associated with Murray.

## **4. Press – Environmental Influences**

**Press:** Environmental forces that influence an individual's behaviour and interact with psychological needs.

Two types:

- **Alpha press:** Objective environmental conditions.
- **Beta press:** An individual's subjective interpretation of environmental conditions.

Example: An examination may objectively be difficult (alpha press), but a student may perceive it as manageable or threatening (beta press).

**NET trap:** Alpha = objective; Beta = perceived.

## **5. Needs–Press Interaction and Thema**

Murray explained behaviour through the interaction of needs and environmental press.

- **Needs:** Internal motivational forces.
- **Press:** External environmental forces.
- **Thema:** A recurring pattern involving the interaction between a person's needs and environmental press.

Thema helps explain recurring patterns in an individual's behaviour and experiences.

- Developed by **Henry Murray and Christiana Morgan (1935)**.
- A projective personality assessment technique.
- Participants construct stories based on ambiguous pictures.
- Stories are analysed for underlying needs, motives, conflicts and environmental pressures.

**Important:** Murray's need theory influenced the interpretation of TAT responses.

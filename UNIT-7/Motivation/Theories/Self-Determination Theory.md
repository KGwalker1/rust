Here are concise, exam-focused notes on **Self-Determination Theory (SDT)** for your NET preparation, continuing the same short and simple format.

### 🎯 Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan)

**1. Core Idea**

- **Definition**: A macro theory of human motivation stating that people have innate growth tendencies and **three basic psychological needs**. The _quality_ of motivation (not just the amount) matters, ranging from controlled to autonomous .
- **Key Insight**: Behavior is self-determined when it is driven by **autonomous motivation** (volition and choice), rather than external pressure or control .

**2. The Three Basic Psychological Needs (Must Know for NET)**

| Need            | Meaning                                                        |
| :-------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Autonomy**    | Feeling in control of one's own actions and choices (volition) |
| **Competence**  | Feeling effective and capable in one's activities              |
| **Relatedness** | Feeling connected to and accepted by others                    |

**3. The Motivation Continuum (Key for NET)**
SDT proposes a continuum from **amotivation** (no motivation) to **intrinsic motivation** (fully self-determined), with different types of extrinsic motivation in between :

- **Amotivation**: Lack of intention to act; feelings of incompetence or helplessness .
- **Extrinsic Motivation**: Doing something for an outcome separate from the activity itself.
- **Intrinsic Motivation**: Doing something for its inherent satisfaction and enjoyment .

**4. Key Concepts to Remember**

- **Cognitive Evaluation Theory (CET)**: A sub-theory explaining how external rewards can **undermine intrinsic motivation** (the "overjustification effect") when they feel controlling rather than informational .
- **Autonomous vs. Controlled Motivation**: Autonomous motivation involves volition and endorsement; controlled motivation involves external pressure or guilt .

**5. Criticisms (Quick Points)**

- **Cultural Bias**: The emphasis on autonomy may reflect individualistic Western values and may not apply equally across collectivist cultures.
- **Oversimplification**: Critics argue needs are broader than the three proposed, and factors like fairness and values also matter .

---

**Exam Tip**: Remember **Deci & Ryan = SDT**. Link the **three needs (Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness)** to the idea that satisfying them enhances autonomous motivation and well-being. Know the continuum from **amotivation → extrinsic → intrinsic**.

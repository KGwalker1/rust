### 🎯 Equity Theory (Adams, 1963/1965)

**1. Core Idea**

- **Definition**: Stacy Adams' theory states that employees are motivated to maintain **fairness (equity)** in the ratio of their **inputs** (effort, skills, time) to **outcomes** (pay, recognition, status) compared to a **referent other** .
- **Key Mechanism**: Perceived inequity creates **tension**, which motivates the person to restore balance. The greater the perceived inequity, the stronger the motivation to reduce it .

**2. The Equity Formula (Must Know for NET)**

- **Comparison**: `(My Outcomes / My Inputs) vs. (Other's Outcomes / Other's Inputs)`
- **Equity exists** when the two ratios are perceived as equal.
- **Inequity exists** when they are unequal (underpayment or overpayment) .

**3. Key Concepts to Remember**

- **Inputs**: What the employee contributes (effort, education, experience, loyalty) .
- **Outcomes**: What the employee receives (salary, benefits, praise, promotion) .
- **Referent Other**: The comparison target (coworker, friend in another company, past self) .
- **Overpayment vs. Underpayment**: Research shows underpayment inequity produces stronger reactions than overpayment. People are more willing to tolerate being overpaid .

**4. Ways to Restore Equity (Adams' 6 Methods)**

1.  **Alter Inputs**: Reduce effort or quality of work.
2.  **Alter Outcomes**: Ask for a raise or better benefits.
3.  **Cognitively Distort Inputs/Outcomes**: Change how you think about them (e.g., "My job isn't that hard anyway").
4.  **Leave the Field**: Quit the job or transfer.
5.  **Change the Referent's Inputs/Outcomes**: Try to get the other person to work harder or earn less.
6.  **Change the Comparison Other**: Choose a different person to compare against.

**5. Criticisms (Quick Points)**

- **Individual Differences Ignored**: Does not account for **equity sensitivity**—some people prefer being overpaid, others prefer equity, and some are "entitleds" who don't care about fairness .
- **Overpayment Effects Weak**: Research has not consistently demonstrated that overpayment actually motivates people to work harder to restore equity .
- **Difficult to Apply**: No clear procedure for organizations to implement the theory in practice .

---

**Exam Tip**: Remember **Adams = Equity Theory**. Link the formula **(Outcomes/Inputs) vs. Referent** to the idea that motivation is driven by **social comparison**, not absolute reward. Know the **6 ways to restore equity** and the key criticism that the theory ignores **individual differences** in fairness sensitivity.

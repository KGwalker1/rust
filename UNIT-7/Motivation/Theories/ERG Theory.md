### 🎯 ERG Theory (Clayton Alderfer)

**1. Core Idea**

- **Definition**: A need theory developed by **Clayton Alderfer** as a revision of Maslow's hierarchy. It condenses five needs into **three core needs**: Existence, Relatedness, and Growth .
- **Key Difference from Maslow**: Needs do **not** have to follow a strict sequence. Multiple needs can be active at the same time, and if a higher-level need is frustrated, a person can **regress** to a lower-level need .

**2. The Three Needs (ERG)**

| Need            | Meaning                                                       | Maslow Equivalent                   |
| :-------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------- |
| **E**xistence   | Basic survival & material needs (food, water, salary, safety) | Physiological + Safety              |
| **R**elatedness | Social relationships, acceptance, and interpersonal ties      | Social + part of Esteem             |
| **G**rowth      | Desire for personal development, creativity, and productivity | Self-actualization + part of Esteem |

**3. Key Propositions (Must Know for NET)**

- **Not a rigid hierarchy**: You don't need to fully satisfy lower needs before pursuing higher ones .
- **Frustration-Regression Principle**: If you are blocked from satisfying a higher-level need (e.g., Growth), you will **regress** and focus more on a lower-level need (e.g., Relatedness or Existence) .
- **Continuum**: The needs form a flexible continuum rather than fixed steps .

**4. ERG vs. Maslow (Quick Contrast)**

- **Maslow**: Rigid, step-by-step progression; once satisfied, a need stops motivating.
- **ERG**: Flexible, can move up and down; a satisfied need can still motivate, and a frustrated high need increases desire for lower needs .

**5. Criticisms (Quick Points)**

- **Less empirical support** than originally hoped; the frustration-regression principle is hard to test definitively.
- **Vagueness**: The boundaries between the three needs are not always clear-cut in practice.

**Exam Tip**: Remember **Alderfer = ERG**. Link **Frustration-Regression** as the key concept that distinguishes it from Maslow. Use the phrase "**flexible hierarchy**" to describe its main advantage over Maslow's rigid model.

# **Michael Ashton & Kibeom Lee – HEXACO Model of Personality**

_UGC-NET JRF Psychology — Short Revision Notes_

## **1. Introduction**

- **Michael C. Ashton** and **Kibeom Lee** jointly developed the **HEXACO six-factor model of personality**.
- It is an alternative to the Big Five model developed through cross-cultural lexical research.
- Their research identified a sixth major personality factor: **Honesty-Humility**.
- Key works:
  - _A Theoretical Basis for the Major Dimensions of Personality_ (2001).
  - _Empirical, Theoretical, and Practical Advantages of the HEXACO Model_ (2007).
  - _The H Factor of Personality_ (2013).
- Key instrument: **HEXACO Personality Inventory-Revised (HEXACO-PI-R)**.

**NET focus:** HEXACO differs from the Big Five by including Honesty-Humility and reorganising the Agreeableness and Emotionality dimensions.

## **2. Core Philosophy – Beyond the Big Five**

### **Lexical Foundation**

- The Big Five was developed largely through English-language lexical research.
- Ashton and Lee examined personality descriptions across multiple languages.
- Cross-language studies identified six broad groups of personality traits.
- The sixth factor was **Honesty-Humility**.

### **Important distinction**

HEXACO does not simply add one factor to the Big Five. It also **repartitions personality-trait content**, particularly in Agreeableness and Emotionality.

Example:

- Big Five: Quick temper is associated with **Neuroticism**.
- HEXACO: Quick temper is associated with **low Agreeableness**.

NET important

&#x20;Remember: HEXACO = Honesty-Humility + reorganisation of Agreeableness and Emotionality.

## **3. The Six HEXACO Factors**

**HEXACO acronym:**

- **H** – Honesty-Humility
- **E** – Emotionality
- **X** – eXtraversion
- **A** – Agreeableness
- **C** – Conscientiousness
- **O** – Openness to Experience

### **High and low scores**

| Factor                | High score                                   | Low score                                    |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Honesty-Humility**  | Sincere, fair, modest, avoids manipulation   | Deceitful, greedy, manipulative, pretentious |
| **Emotionality**      | Anxious, fearful, sentimental, needs support | Calm, fearless, emotionally independent      |
| **Extraversion**      | Sociable, confident, energetic               | Reserved, socially uncomfortable, low energy |
| **Agreeableness**     | Forgiving, gentle, patient, cooperative      | Critical, stubborn, easily angered           |
| **Conscientiousness** | Organised, disciplined, careful              | Careless, impulsive, disorganised            |
| **Openness**          | Creative, curious, imaginative               | Conventional, incurious, less creative       |

**Important:** Low Honesty-Humility is associated with the Dark Triad:

1. Psychopathy
2. Machiavellianism
3. Narcissism

## **4. HEXACO-PI-R – 24 Facets**

The HEXACO-PI-R measures **6 factors, with 4 facets each = 24 facets**.

| Factor                    | Four facets                                                            |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Honesty-Humility (H)**  | Sincerity, Fairness, Greed Avoidance, Modesty                          |
| **Emotionality (E)**      | Fearfulness, Anxiety, Dependence, Sentimentality                       |
| **Extraversion (X)**      | Social Self-Esteem, Social Boldness, Sociability, Liveliness           |
| **Agreeableness (A)**     | Forgivingness, Gentleness, Flexibility, Patience                       |
| **Conscientiousness (C)** | Organization, Diligence, Perfectionism, Prudence                       |
| **Openness (O)**          | Aesthetic Appreciation, Inquisitiveness, Creativity, Unconventionality |

### **Interstitial facet**

**Altruism (vs. Antagonism)**

- An additional facet that cuts across Honesty-Humility, Emotionality and Agreeableness.
- It is not one of the four regular facets within a single factor.
- Thus, HEXACO has 24 regular facets plus this interstitial facet.

## **5. Theoretical Basis – Altruism and Engagement**

Ashton and Lee proposed an evolutionary-functional explanation for the six dimensions.

### **A. Altruism**

Three factors are associated with altruistic tendencies:

| Factor               | Main function                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Honesty-Humility** | Fairness and sincerity; cooperation rather than exploitation |
| **Agreeableness**    | Forgiveness and tolerance                                    |
| **Emotionality**     | Empathy, attachment and kin altruism                         |

- Honesty-Humility and Agreeableness relate to **reciprocal altruism**.
- Emotionality relates to **kin altruism**.

### **B. Engagement**

Three factors represent engagement in different areas:

| Factor                | Engagement domain       |
| --------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Extraversion**      | Social engagement       |
| **Conscientiousness** | Task-related engagement |
| **Openness**          | Idea-related engagement |

**NET keyword:** HEXACO's theoretical framework = **Altruism and Engagement**.

## **6. HEXACO vs. Big Five**

| Feature             | Big Five                  | HEXACO                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Number of factors   | 5                         | **6**                                         |
| Unique factor       | None corresponding to H   | **Honesty-Humility**                          |
| Emotional dimension | Neuroticism               | Emotionality                                  |
| Quick temper        | Neuroticism               | Low Agreeableness                             |
| Factor organisation | OCEAN                     | HEXACO                                        |
| Dark Triad          | Less directly represented | Low Honesty-Humility                          |
| Workplace deviance  | Predictive                | Honesty-Humility provides stronger prediction |

- HEXACO's Honesty-Humility is particularly useful in predicting unethical behaviour and workplace deviance.
- The supplied source reports a correlation of **ρ = −0.482** for Honesty-Humility and workplace deviance.

## **8. Contributions**

1. Identified **Honesty-Humility** as a major personality dimension.
2. Developed the HEXACO-PI-R to measure six personality factors.
3. Provided cross-language lexical evidence for a six-factor structure.
4. Improved prediction of workplace deviance and unethical behaviour.
5. Proposed an evolutionary-functional explanation based on altruism and engagement.

## **9. Criticisms**

1. **Overlap with Agreeableness:** Honesty-Humility correlates with Big Five Agreeableness, raising questions about its distinctiveness.
2. **Inconsistent replication:** Not all lexical studies identify six factors.
3. **Competing models:** Other six-factor models do not necessarily identify the same dimensions.
4. **Limited clinical use:** HEXACO is less widely used in clinical settings than established personality inventories.
5. **Reorganisation:** The changed Agreeableness and Emotionality dimensions complicate direct comparisons with the Big Five.
